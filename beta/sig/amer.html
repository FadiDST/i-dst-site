2. Paste it into Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on a computer, signed in as amer@i-dst.com.
  2. Click the gear (top right) → See all settings → General tab.
  3. Scroll to Signature → Create new → name it i-DST.
  4. Click in the empty box and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on Mac).
  5. Under Signature defaults, pick i-DST for both new emails and on reply/forward.
  6. Tick “Insert signature before quoted text … and remove the ‘--’ line”.
  7. Scroll to the very bottom → Save Changes. Done.

On the phone apps, Gmail uses a plain text signature. Leave it as it is.
