Privacy policy

Version 1.0, 25 November 2026. Replaces the privacy notice version 0.1 of 17 November 2026.

This policy explains what personal data Intelligent Distribution (i-DST) collects, why, who helps us process it and the rights you have. It covers our website i-dst.com, our booking page i-dst.com/go, our digital business cards, our WhatsApp line, our phone calls and emails, and the events where we meet you.

Who we are

Intelligent Distribution (i-DST), Amman, Jordan, is responsible for your personal data. Our company registration in Jordan is in progress. We will add our registered name, number and address here as soon as it is complete.

Contact for anything about your data: privacy@i-dst.com

What we do not do

  • We do not record calls.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use analytics tools. See Cookies and storage.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with the manufacturers we work with unless you ask us to or agree first.
  • Our engineers talk to you, not a machine. We do not use AI to talk to customers, and we make no automated decisions about you with legal or similar effect.

Transfers outside your country

Most of our providers are in the United States. We transfer data only with the safeguards in their terms (for example standard contractual clauses for the EU and UK) and, where Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law or your local law requires it, with your consent, which you give when you send us a request.

How we protect it

Only our team can see your data, each person with a personal PIN. Data travels encrypted. We would tell you and the authorities about a breach as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time, without cost and without affecting anything else between us. Email privacy@i-dst.com. We reply within 30 days.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Jordan, the Personal Data Protection Council at the Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship; elsewhere, the authority in your country.

Laws we follow

Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023, and where they apply, the EU General Data Protection Regulation and the data protection laws of the countries we serve.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

We will post any change here with a new version number and date.
