Cookies and storage

Version 1.1, 27 November 2026.

Short version: no advertising cookies, no tracking, no analytics. Our website only remembers a few choices you make, in your own browser.

What your browser keeps

NameWhat it remembersHow long
idst-langYour language: English, Arabic, French or SpanishUntil you clear it
idst-countryYour country, so we show our calling hours in your timeUntil you clear it
idst-themeLight or dark modeUntil you clear it
idst-cursor, idst-cursor-sizeThe cursor and cursor size you pickedUntil you clear it
idst-playerThe name you last used for the top scores, so you do not type it againUntil you clear it
idst-lb-*A copy of your scores in our games, kept on your deviceUntil you clear it
idst_okThat you entered the preview password (only while the site is in preview)Until you close the tab or clear it

These are stored with your browser's local storage, not cookies. They never leave your device and we cannot read them. These choices are needed for the site to work as you set it, so we do not ask for consent first.

Things other companies see

  • Fonts: the website serves its fonts from i-dst.com itself; no font request goes to Google.
  • Event video: the interpack MEA video loads from the organizer's website.
  • WhatsApp: when you tap a WhatsApp button, WhatsApp opens under Meta's own terms.

How to clear it

Clear your browser's site data for i-dst.com, or use a private window.
